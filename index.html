Creamy White
Sauce Pasta

Silky, cheesy, and full of cozy flavor. This simple white sauce pasta is the kind of feel-good meal everyone will want seconds of.

Explore the recipe ↓
◷ Prep time 10 mins
♨ Cook time 20 mins
♧ Servings 2 people
✳ Difficulty Easy
01

What you'll need

Simple ingredients, a delicious result.

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Mixed herbs and chili flakes
  • Chopped capsicum (optional)
02

Let's make it

Follow along, one easy step at a time.

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until cooked, then drain it.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and sauté chopped garlic.
  3. Add all-purpose flour and stir continuously for a minute.
  4. Gradually add milk while stirring to avoid lumps.
  5. Cook until the sauce becomes thick and creamy.
  6. Add grated cheese, salt, pepper, and mixed herbs.
  7. Add the boiled pasta and optional capsicum.
  8. Mix everything well and serve hot.
✦

A little kitchen secret

Add a splash of pasta water if the sauce becomes too thick. It helps bring everything together.
